- Include a short buying advice section after the picks.
- End with a clear top recommendation and runner-up. Wrap the final recommendation summary in Markdown blockquote syntax (`>`) and use ratings stars or `✅` lists to highlight recommendations. Do not use the heading "Conclusion", "Summary", or "Final Thoughts".

STYLE:

- Do not make every pick sound equally perfect.
